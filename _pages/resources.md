## Recommended textbooks

- *Measuring Biological Diversity*, Magurran (2004)
- *Biological Diversity*, Magurran and McGill (2011)
- *Numerical Ecology*, Legendre and Legendre (2013)
- *Introductory Statistics with R*, Dalgaard (2008)
- *Numerical Ecology with R*, Borcard, Gillet, and Legendre (2011)
- *Functional and Phylogenetic Ecology in R*, Swenson (2014)
- *Phylogenies in Ecology*, Cadotte and Davies (2016)
- [*Pro Git*, Chacon and Straub](https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2)

## Computational approaches to biodiversity using R and GitHub

We use [R](https://www.r-project.org/) for statistical computing and data visualization, [RStudio](https://posit.co/products/open-source/rstudio/) as a working environment, and [GitHub](https://github.com/QuantitativeBiodiversity) to track and collaborate on course projects. Students use their own GitHub repositories for work and submissions.
